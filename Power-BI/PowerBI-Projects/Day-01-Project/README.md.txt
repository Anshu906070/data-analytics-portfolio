#  Day 01 – Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes sales performance using an interactive dashboard. It helps identify top-performing cities, product categories, and products while providing actionable business insights.

---

## Tools Used

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- CSV Dataset

---

##  Dataset

- Dataset: sniffoss.csv
- Records: 1000

---

##  KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Revenue
- Number of Products
- Number of Cities

---

## Dashboard Visuals

- KPI Cards
- Sales by City
- Sales by Category
- Top Products
- Revenue Trend
- Interactive Filters

## Business Insights

- Chennai generated the highest revenue.
- Bangalore generated the lowest revenue.
- Clothing is the highest-performing category.
- Electronics ranks second in revenue.
- Phone is the top-selling product.
- Bread is the second-highest revenue product.
